Industrial Welding & Fabrication Capabilities

At A Step Above Industrial Inc., our fabrication shop is equipped to handle projects of all sizes—from precision repairs and custom fabrications to large-scale industrial components and complex engineered projects.

Our experienced Red Seal tradespeople bring a high level of craftsmanship, technical knowledge, and attention to detail to every project. With professional welding capabilities, fabrication equipment, and the ability to work from engineered drawings and specifications, we provide reliable solutions for industrial, commercial, and custom applications.

Whether you require a one-off custom component, structural fabrication, production work, or large-scale industrial assemblies, our team has the skills and resources to deliver quality workmanship on schedule.

From concept to completion, A Step Above Industrial Inc. combines skilled fabrication, engineering collaboration, and industrial experience to build solutions that are safe, durable, and designed to perform.
