Each bearing is carefully protected by wrapping it with a brand-new, heavy-duty 8mm industrial protective sleeve, which is then securely taped in place. This prevents even the smallest particles of aluminum dust or cleaning debris from coming into contact with the bearings. By taking this extra precaution, we ensure that the bearings remain in the same pristine operating condition as when we first received the steps, guaranteeing long-term reliability and performance.
