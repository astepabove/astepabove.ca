A Step Above Industrial Inc. provides comprehensive industrial installation services with the experience and capability to handle complex mechanical, structural, and equipment installation projects.

Our team specializes in the installation, modification, and upgrading of industrial systems, including dust collection systems, process equipment, truck service bodies, motors, gearboxes, pumps, conveyors, and custom mechanical assemblies. From structural fabrication and equipment placement to precision alignment and final commissioning, we deliver complete solutions built for reliability and long-term performance.

With extensive experience in millwrighting, welding, fabrication, and industrial maintenance, we understand the importance of precision, safety, and minimizing operational downtime. Our team is skilled in equipment installation, shaft and coupling alignment, motor and gearbox setup, mechanical troubleshooting, structural modifications, and custom-built solutions designed to meet the demands of industrial environments.

Safety and professionalism are at the core of every project. Our personnel are trained and experienced with industry-standard requirements, including:

- Occupational Health & Safety (OH&S) compliance
- WHMIS
- Fall Protection
- Confined Space Awareness
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures
- Elevated Work Platforms (EWP)
- Mobile Equipment Awareness
- Rigging and Hoisting practices
- Crane and lifting safety
- Welding and hot work safety
- Respirator and PPE requirements
- First Aid and site-specific safety orientations

Whether upgrading an existing facility, installing new equipment, or completing a challenging shutdown project, A Step Above Industrial Inc. brings the craftsmanship, technical knowledge, and attention to detail required for high-performance industrial operations.

From structural steel to precision mechanical systems, we provide dependable installation solutions that keep industry moving.
