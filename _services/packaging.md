All refurbished steps are professionally packaged to ensure safe transport. Each unit is carefully wrapped in multiple layers of industrial-grade stretch film, providing a durable protective barrier against dirt, moisture, and handling damage. The steps are then securely fastened to the pallet using high-strength banding applied with a commercial banding machine. This process guarantees that every step remains firmly in place throughout the entire shipment, arriving at its destination in excellent condition and ready for immediate use.
