A Step Above Industrial Inc. provides responsive, professional on-site service through our dedicated fleet of fully equipped service trucks. Each unit is outfitted with welding equipment, fabrication tools, and the specialized gear required to handle a wide range of industrial repairs, maintenance, and custom field projects.

Whether responding to an unexpected breakdown or completing a scheduled maintenance project, our experienced team is prepared to deliver efficient solutions that help minimize downtime and keep your operations moving.

Customer satisfaction is at the core of everything we do. When a challenge extends beyond our capabilities, we rely on our trusted network of industry professionals to connect you with the right resources and ensure the job gets done properly.

From emergency response to planned industrial support, A Step Above Industrial Inc. is committed to providing dependable service, quality workmanship, and solutions you can count on.
