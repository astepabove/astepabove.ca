## Tread Grooving

In our welding and machining department we repair tread grooves of any depth with absolute precision. Even in cases where the majority of the surface is compromised, we restore the tread to its original profile and machine-grade finish. After repair, the grooves are fully rebuilt, leaving no visible signs of damage or welding. The result is a flawless surface that meets or exceeds original manufacturing standards.

## Screw Damage

We repair every type of tread damage with precision. Whether the wear is on the surface or the tread sides, it’s no challenge for us. Using state-of-the-art semi-automated welding equipment, we completely rebuild the tread back to a machine-grade finish and surface plane. High grade filler metal is engineered to exceed the strength and durability of the original casting, ensuring a longer service life than the parent material itself.

With our process, there’s no risk of porosity, no cold laps, and no visible weld seams on the tread sides. Once refinished and painted, even we struggle to identify where the repairs were made—and that’s exactly how it should be.
