Each escalator step undergoes a meticulous, multi-stage detailing process designed to restore it to a like-new condition. All debris, contaminants, and build-up are carefully removed, including dirt, grease, and grime that accumulate over time. Our detailing department ensure that every part of the step—inside, outside, and across all surfaces—is thoroughly cleaned, leaving no area overlooked. This attention to detail not only improves the appearance of the step but also enhances safety and prepares it for any further repair or finishing work.
